## Hi there, im Lara 👋

**Physician & Biomedical Data Science Researcher**

My research combines clinical hematology, molecular profiling and computational approaches to investigate acute myeloid leukemia and other myeloid malignancies.

## Research Areas

### 🧬 Molecular Characterization of Myeloid Malignancies
Mutational landscapes, molecular subtypes and their associations with clinical characteristics and outcomes.

### 🔬 Cellular Heterogeneity & RNA Biology
Single-cell transcriptomics, cellular states and alternative RNA splicing in myeloid malignancies.

### 🔀 Clonal Evolution & Disease Progression
Longitudinal genomic and transcriptomic changes associated with disease progression, relapse and therapy resistance.
